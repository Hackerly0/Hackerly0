About

A seasoned academic and researcher with over 25 years of experience in electrical and communications engineering. Holds a Ph.D. from the National University of Malaysia in microwave propagation modelling, and an M.Sc. from the University of Texas at Arlington in optical communications. Author of more than 20 peer-reviewed publications, with research spanning path-loss modelling, 5G channel characterisation, space-time block coding, and sound source localisation. Active IEEE volunteer, currently serving as Vice Chair of IEEE COMSOC Jordan Section.
